# Images

Place your images here with these exact filenames:

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `logo.png` | Your Dean's Petals & Produce logo (shown in header) |
| `stand1.jpg` | Hero photo of the farm stand (fills the left half of the page) |

Both images are optional — the site degrades gracefully if they're missing.

**Tips:**
- `stand1.jpg` looks best as a landscape photo (wider than tall).
  Ideal size: 1400 × 1600 px or similar tall-ish crop.
- `logo.png` should have a transparent background if possible.
